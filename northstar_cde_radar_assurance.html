CDE Radar + Assurance

A project team submits an API spec. Radar finds which fields are critical data elements, reconciles them with the application’s CDE inventory, and Assurance proves — clause by clause — whether the required evidence is present and current. The steward gets one review packet with every finding, its evidence, its owner and its next action.

Business objective

Discover critical data at design time and prove it is controlled before release.

Fewer CDEs found lateCandidates surface when the API is drafted, not at release review.
Tags that are actually rightWrong-application, duplicate, expired or changed tags ask for review.
Evidence, not assertionsEach clause returns PASS, FAIL or UNKNOWN with the declared evidence.

Self-checks for this component: — passing

The problem

Late discovery

Criticality found after design

CDE definitions live in catalogs and policies, not in the design. Important fields ship without an owner, lineage or tested controls.

Tags ≠ readiness

An approved tag says nothing about today

A field approved last year can have missing lineage, a failed quality test and stale evidence.

Institutional knowledge

Schemas alone cannot answer it

A schema-only classifier cannot know what is tagged or what a policy demands. Inventory and policy access is the differentiator.

Four decisions per field

Kept separate — each has a different owner.

01 · PRESENCE

Is there critical data?

Approved CDEs or new candidates in the submitted scope?

02 · MAPPING

Does the tag really match?

Same field, application, type and an unexpired approval?

03 · READINESS

Is the evidence current?

Owner, source, lineage, passing test, evidence ≤ 30 days?

04 · ACTION

Who fixes what?

Steward, control owner or data owner — and what goes in the contract?
